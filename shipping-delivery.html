SHIPPING & DELIVERY

Last updated: September 2026

Orders are processed after successful payment confirmation. Dispatch and delivery estimates shown at checkout are estimates, not guarantees, and depend on serviceability, courier operations, weather, and other factors. We currently ship within India only.

Each bottle is carefully packed to reduce the risk of breakage or leakage in transit. If your order arrives with visible damage to the outer packaging, please note this at the time of delivery where possible, and see our Cancellation & Refund policy for how to report a damaged, incorrect, or defective item.

Please provide a complete, accurate delivery address, city, PIN code, email address, and phone number. We will contact you if an address requires clarification. Shipping charges, serviceable areas, dispatch times, and tracking details will be confirmed at checkout when launch operations are finalised.
