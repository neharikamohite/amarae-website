CANCELLATION & REFUND

Last updated: September 2026

Cancelling an order

To request a cancellation before dispatch, email help@amaraeformulations.com with your order number as soon as possible. We will confirm by email whether cancellation is still possible.

What's eligible for a refund

Refunds and replacements are available only when an order arrives damaged, incorrect, or defective. Once a fragrance bottle has been opened or used, it is not eligible for return, exchange, or refund for any other reason — this is a hygiene and safety requirement and applies regardless of the reason for return.

How to request one

Contact help@amaraeformulations.com within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos or video of the unopened outer package and the item, taken before discarding any packaging. We will review your request and confirm the outcome by email, and arrange a replacement or refund where appropriate.

How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are reviewed manually and issued to your original payment method only, through our payment provider (Razorpay) — we are unable to refund to a different bank account, UPI ID, or card, or via cash or direct bank transfer, in line with standard payment regulations. Once a refund is initiated, funds typically reflect in your account within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

This policy will be updated with final launch timelines once operations are confirmed.
