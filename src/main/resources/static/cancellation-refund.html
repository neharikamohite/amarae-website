CANCELLATION & REFUND

Last updated: August 2026

To request a cancellation before dispatch, email help@amaraeformulations.com with your order number as soon as possible. We will confirm whether cancellation remains possible.

For hygiene and safety, opened fragrance products are generally not eligible for return. If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or materially defective, contact us promptly with your order number and clear photos or video of the unopened package and item. We will assess the request and, where appropriate, arrange a replacement or refund.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing time depends on the payment provider and bank. This policy will be updated with final launch timelines once operations are confirmed.
