SHIPPING & DELIVERY

Last updated: September 2026

AMARAÈ Formulations currently ships only within India. At checkout we validate your PIN code and mobile number to confirm the address is serviceable within India before payment is taken — orders cannot be placed with a non-Indian address at this time.

Shipping is calculated automatically at checkout: orders below ₹1,499 carry a flat ₹79 shipping charge, and orders of ₹1,499 or more ship free. The exact shipping charge and order total are shown on the cart page before you pay.

Orders are processed after successful payment confirmation and typically dispatched within 2-4 business days. Delivery estimates shown at checkout are estimates, not guarantees, and depend on serviceability, courier operations, weather, and other factors.

Please provide a complete, accurate delivery address, city, state, PIN code, email address, and phone number at checkout. We will contact you if an address requires clarification.

For delivery assistance, email help@amaraeformulations.com or call +91 95792 22532.
